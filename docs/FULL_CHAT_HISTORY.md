### 1. Market Regime Classification (Detecting the "Weather")
Rather than guessing where price goes, the engine classifies the market into **4 mathematical states** using volatility ($\sigma$), spread width, and price velocity:

```
                  ┌─────────────── Market Regimes ───────────────┐
                  │                                              │
    [CALM]        │ Low volatility (<1.2%), tight spread, stable │ Strategy: Mean Reversion / Grid
    [TRENDING]    │ Directional volume push, sustained momentum  │ Strategy: Breakout / Trend-Following
    [JITTERY]     │ Erratic chop, sudden spikes, false breakouts │ Strategy: Sit in Cash / Cut Size 50%
    [CRASH]       │ Bids vanish, spread explodes, heavy selloff  │ Strategy: 100% Cash / Circuit Breaker
```

### 2. Order Book Imbalance (OBI — Reading Microstructure)
Before touching price charts, the engine inspects the **top of the order book** (the bids and asks sitting on the exchange waiting to get filled):

$$\text{OBI} = \frac{\text{Volume}_{\text{Bids}} - \text{Volume}_{\text{Asks}}}{\text{Volume}_{\text{Bids}} + \text{Volume}_{\text{Asks}}}$$

* **$\text{OBI} > +0.35$ (Heavy Buying Wall)**: More capital is bidding to buy than sell. Price has upward pressure.
* **$\text{OBI} < -0.35$ (Heavy Selling Wall)**: Asks outnumber bids. Downward pressure.
* **Spread Monitoring**: If the bid-ask gap widens beyond $0.05\%$, liquidity is drying up. The engine pauses orders to avoid losing money to slippage.

---

### 3. Quarter-Kelly Position Sizing (Risking the Right Amount)
Amateur traders gamble fixed $10\%$ or $20\%$ per trade. Quants use the **Kelly Criterion**, which calculates the mathematically optimal bet size:

$$f^* = \frac{b \cdot p - (1 - p)}{b}$$

*(where $p = \text{win rate}$, $b = \text{reward-to-risk ratio}$)*

* **Why Full Kelly Kills Accounts**: Full Kelly assumes normal distributions. Crypto has "fat tails" (black swan dumps). 
* **The Solution — Quarter-Kelly ($f^* / 4$)**: We size down to 25% of theoretical Kelly:
  * In a high-confidence `TRENDING` regime with $+0.50$ OBI $\to$ Allocates up to $8\%\text{--}12\%$ of bankroll.
  * In a `JITTERY` regime $\to$ Scales down to $2\%\text{--}3\%$ or zero.

---

### 4. The 15% Handbrake (High-Water Mark Circuit Breaker)
This is the uncompromised fail-safe:

$$\text{Drawdown} = \frac{\text{Peak Balance (High-Water Mark)} - \text{Current Balance}}{\text{Peak Balance}}$$

```
  $5,000 (Peak HWM)
     │
     ▼ -15% Drawdown Level ($4,250)
  ───┼────────────────────────────────────────── [CIRCUIT BREAKER HARD TRIP]
     │                                            • Closes all open inventory
     │                                            • Freezes new order placement
     │                                            • Requires human override to reset
```

* **Checked BEFORE Every Order**: Most bots blow up because they keep trading during an exchange outage, flash depeg, or cascading liquidation.
